Built for a little daily practice.

Hanzi Learning helps you review Chinese vocabulary and practice writing characters, one section at a time.

Your learning session

You can browse textbooks and practice without an account. Completed practice scores are saved only in this browser, up to 200 sessions. View, export, or clear them in My progress. The app does not send scores or answers to the server.

Administrator access

Signing in creates a session cookie that lasts up to one hour. It is used to protect the textbook editor. The server stores failed PIN attempts and cooldowns to prevent repeated guessing.

External resources

Mozilla Text and Google Material Symbols fonts are served by this site. Stroke animations and character data load from jsDelivr. The feedback form is hosted by Google; opening it connects to Google.

Learning materials

Vocabulary and definitions are provided as study aids. If you find a mistake, please send feedback.
